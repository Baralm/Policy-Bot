# Brake and Tire Safety Standard

**Doc ID:** DOC-002
**Version:** 2.1
**Effective Date:** 2025-03-01
**Owner:** Fleet Engineering
**Applies to:** All fleet vehicles, including electric and hybrid vehicles

---

### [DOC-002] Section 1 - Purpose and Relationship to Other Documents
1.1 This standard sets minimum safety requirements for brakes and tires.
1.2 This standard updates the brake and tire requirements of the Fleet Preventive Maintenance Policy (DOC-001). Topics not covered here continue to follow DOC-001.

### [DOC-002] Section 2.1 - Brake Inspection Interval
Brakes must be inspected every 15,000 km or 6 months, whichever comes first.

### [DOC-002] Section 2.2 - Severe Service Brake Inspection
Vehicles in severe service must have brakes inspected every 10,000 km. Severe service means predominantly urban stop-and-go driving, regular towing, or regular operation on mountain routes.

### [DOC-002] Section 2.3 - Brake Pad and Lining Limits
Brake pads must be replaced when friction material reaches 3 mm. Drum brake linings must be replaced at 2 mm. Rotors and drums must be replaced when below the manufacturer's minimum thickness marking.

### [DOC-002] Section 2.4 - Brake Fluid
Brake fluid must be replaced every 2 years, or immediately if a moisture test shows more than 3% water content.

### [DOC-002] Section 3.1 - Tire Tread Depth
Steer axle tires must be replaced when tread depth reaches 4 mm. All other tire positions must be replaced when tread depth reaches 3 mm.

### [DOC-002] Section 3.2 - Tire Pressure
Tire pressure must be checked weekly with tires cold and kept within plus or minus 5% of the pressure on the vehicle placard.

### [DOC-002] Section 3.3 - Tire Age
Tires older than 6 years from the manufacture date code must be replaced regardless of tread depth.

### [DOC-002] Section 3.4 - Retreaded Tires
Retreaded tires are prohibited on steer axles. Retreads may be used on drive and trailer axles of medium-duty trucks only.

### [DOC-002] Section 4 - Out-of-Service Conditions
A vehicle must be taken out of service immediately if any of the following is found: brake warning light on, brake pad below 3 mm, audible air leak in the brake system, tire cord or steel belt exposed, tire sidewall bulge or cut, or tread below the limits in Section 3.1.

### [DOC-002] Section 5 - Wheel Nut Re-Torque
After any wheel is removed and refitted, wheel nuts must be re-torqued to specification after 50 to 100 km of driving. The re-torque must be recorded in FleetTrack.
