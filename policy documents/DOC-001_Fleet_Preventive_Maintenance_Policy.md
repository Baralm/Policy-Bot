# Fleet Preventive Maintenance Policy

**Doc ID:** DOC-001
**Version:** 3.0
**Effective Date:** 2023-01-15
**Owner:** Fleet Operations
**Applies to:** All company-owned and leased vehicles

---

### [DOC-001] Section 1 - Purpose
This policy defines the baseline preventive maintenance (PM) requirements for the company fleet to keep vehicles safe, reliable, and compliant, and to control maintenance cost.

### [DOC-001] Section 2 - Scope
2.1 This policy applies to all light-duty vans, pickup trucks, and medium-duty trucks owned or leased by the company.
2.2 Electric and hybrid vehicles must also follow the EV and Hybrid Maintenance Addendum (DOC-005) for high-voltage and powertrain items.

### [DOC-001] Section 3 - Document Precedence
3.1 This policy is the baseline document of the Fleet Policy Library.
3.2 Where another approved document in the Fleet Policy Library addresses the same requirement and conflicts with this policy, the document with the later effective date prevails for that requirement.
3.3 Vehicle-type addenda prevail over general documents for the vehicle types they cover.
3.4 Questions about precedence that cannot be resolved by Sections 3.2 and 3.3 must be referred to the Fleet Manager.

### [DOC-001] Section 4.1 - Engine Oil and Filter
Engine oil and oil filter must be changed every 10,000 km or 6 months, whichever comes first. Medium-duty diesel trucks may extend this to 15,000 km when using full synthetic oil approved by Fleet Engineering.

### [DOC-001] Section 4.2 - Brake Inspection Interval
Brakes must be inspected every 20,000 km or 12 months, whichever comes first. The inspection includes pads or shoes, rotors or drums, hoses, and fluid level.

### [DOC-001] Section 4.3 - Tire Rotation
Tires must be rotated every 15,000 km. Directional tires must be rotated front-to-rear on the same side only.

### [DOC-001] Section 4.4 - Tire Tread Depth
Tires must be replaced when tread depth reaches 3 mm at any point across the tread, on all wheel positions.

### [DOC-001] Section 4.5 - Coolant
Engine coolant must be flushed and replaced every 2 years. Coolant level and condition are checked at every PM service.

### [DOC-001] Section 4.6 - Air Filter
The engine air filter is inspected at every PM service and replaced every 30,000 km, or earlier if visibly clogged.

### [DOC-001] Section 4.7 - 12V Battery
The 12V battery must be load-tested every 12 months. Batteries that fail the load test must be replaced before the vehicle returns to service.

### [DOC-001] Section 5 - Overdue Preventive Maintenance
5.1 A vehicle that is more than 1,000 km or 14 days overdue for scheduled PM is flagged as "PM Overdue" in FleetTrack and the assigned driver's manager is notified.
5.2 A vehicle that is more than 2,500 km or 30 days overdue must be removed from service until the PM is completed.

### [DOC-001] Section 6 - Defect Reporting
Drivers must report vehicle defects to the fleet office within 48 hours of discovery.

### [DOC-001] Section 7 - Maintenance Records
7.1 All maintenance and repair work must be entered in FleetTrack within 2 business days of completion.
7.2 Maintenance records must be retained for a minimum of 3 years.

### [DOC-001] Section 8 - Repair Approval Limits
8.1 Repairs estimated above $2,500 require Fleet Manager approval before work begins.
8.2 Repairs estimated above $10,000 require approval from the Director of Operations before work begins.
8.3 Emergency roadside repairs up to $1,000 may be authorized by Fleet Dispatch.

### [DOC-001] Section 9 - External Vendors
Vehicles may only be serviced by external repair shops listed on the Approved Vendor List. Use of any other vendor requires written Fleet Manager approval.
