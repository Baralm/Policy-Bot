# Driver Daily Inspection and Defect Reporting Procedure

**Doc ID:** DOC-003
**Version:** 1.4
**Effective Date:** 2024-06-10
**Owner:** Safety and Compliance
**Applies to:** All drivers and technicians

---

### [DOC-003] Section 1 - Purpose
This procedure describes how drivers inspect vehicles each day and how defects are reported, classified, repaired, and verified.

### [DOC-003] Section 2 - Pre-Trip Inspection
2.1 Drivers must complete a pre-trip inspection in the FleetTrack mobile app before the first trip of each day.
2.2 The pre-trip inspection covers: tires and wheels, brakes (including parking brake), steering, lights and indicators, mirrors and windshield, wipers, horn, fluid leaks, dashboard warning lights, and cargo securement equipment.
2.3 A pre-trip inspection takes approximately 10 to 15 minutes and is paid working time.

### [DOC-003] Section 3 - Post-Trip Inspection
At the end of each shift the driver must complete a post-trip inspection in FleetTrack, recording fuel or charge level, odometer reading, and any defects found during the shift.

### [DOC-003] Section 4.1 - Critical Defects
Critical defects are defects affecting brakes, steering, tires, lights, fuel leaks, or any high-voltage warning on electric or hybrid vehicles. A vehicle with a critical defect must not be operated. The driver must report it immediately by phone to Fleet Dispatch and record it in FleetTrack.

### [DOC-003] Section 4.2 - Non-Critical Defects
Non-critical defects are all other defects, for example interior trim damage, a faulty radio, or minor body damage. Non-critical defects must be reported in FleetTrack before the end of the shift in which they are found.

### [DOC-003] Section 5 - Red Tag Procedure
When a critical defect is reported, the driver or supervisor must place a red "DO NOT OPERATE" tag on the steering wheel. Only a qualified technician may remove the red tag, after the repair is completed and verified.

### [DOC-003] Section 6 - Repair Verification
6.1 The technician must sign off each repaired defect in FleetTrack, describing the work performed.
6.2 The driver must acknowledge the repair during the next pre-trip inspection. If the driver believes the defect is not fixed, the vehicle is re-tagged and returned to the workshop.

### [DOC-003] Section 7 - Inspection Report Retention
Completed pre-trip and post-trip inspection reports must be retained for 12 months.

### [DOC-003] Section 8 - Non-Compliance
Failure to complete a required inspection or to report a known defect is a safety violation and is handled under the Driver Disciplinary Procedure.
