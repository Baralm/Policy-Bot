# Workshop Safety and Lockout/Tagout Policy

**Doc ID:** DOC-004
**Version:** 2.0
**Effective Date:** 2024-09-01
**Owner:** Environment, Health and Safety (EHS)
**Applies to:** All company maintenance workshops and technicians

---

### [DOC-004] Section 1 - Scope
This policy applies to all work performed in company maintenance workshops, including by contractors working on company premises.

### [DOC-004] Section 2 - Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
2.1 Safety glasses must be worn at all times in the workshop bay area.
2.2 Steel-toe safety boots are mandatory in the workshop.
2.3 Nitrile gloves must be worn when handling oils, coolant, brake fluid, or fuel.
2.4 Hearing protection must be worn in areas where noise exceeds 85 dBA, including when using impact tools.

### [DOC-004] Section 3.1 - Lockout/Tagout Procedure
Before any maintenance work begins, the technician must: switch off the vehicle, remove the key and place it in the lockout key box, apply a personal lock and a tag showing their name and date, and, for any electrical work, disconnect the 12V battery negative terminal.

### [DOC-004] Section 3.2 - Lock Removal
Only the technician who applied a lock may remove it. If that technician is unavailable, the lock may be removed only by the Workshop Supervisor after confirming the technician is not on site and the vehicle is safe.

### [DOC-004] Section 3.3 - High-Voltage Systems
Lockout of high-voltage systems on electric and hybrid vehicles requires additional steps defined in the EV and Hybrid Maintenance Addendum (DOC-005).

### [DOC-004] Section 4 - Vehicle Lifts and Supports
4.1 Vehicle lifts must be inspected annually by a certified lift inspector and visually checked by the user before each day of use.
4.2 No one may work under a vehicle supported only by a jack. Rated jack stands or a lift with locks engaged must be used.
4.3 Lifts that fail inspection must be tagged out of use until repaired.

### [DOC-004] Section 5 - Hazardous Waste
5.1 Used oil must be stored in labeled, closed containers in the designated waste area.
5.2 Used oil filters must be hot-drained for at least 12 hours before disposal.
5.3 Used coolant, brake fluid, and lead-acid batteries must be collected separately for recycling.
5.4 Any spill larger than 20 litres must be reported to EHS within 1 hour.

### [DOC-004] Section 6 - Incident Reporting
All injuries must be reported to the supervisor before the end of the shift. Near misses must be reported to EHS within 24 hours.

### [DOC-004] Section 7 - Training
7.1 New technicians must complete the workshop safety induction before working unsupervised.
7.2 Workshop safety refresher training is required every 2 years.
