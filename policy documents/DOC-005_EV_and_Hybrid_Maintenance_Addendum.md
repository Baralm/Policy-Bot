# EV and Hybrid Maintenance and High-Voltage Safety Addendum

**Doc ID:** DOC-005
**Version:** 1.1
**Effective Date:** 2025-08-01
**Owner:** Fleet Engineering
**Applies to:** Battery electric vehicles (BEV), plug-in hybrids (PHEV), and hybrids (HEV)

---

### [DOC-005] Section 1 - Scope and Relationship to Other Documents
1.1 This addendum supplements the Fleet Preventive Maintenance Policy (DOC-001) for electric and hybrid vehicles.
1.2 Where this addendum conflicts with any other fleet policy document, this addendum prevails for electric and hybrid vehicles.

### [DOC-005] Section 2 - Technician Qualification
2.1 Only technicians holding High-Voltage (HV) Level 2 certification or higher may work on HV components, which are identified by orange cables and HV warning labels.
2.2 All workshop staff must complete HV Level 1 awareness training.
2.3 HV certification must be renewed every 3 years.

### [DOC-005] Section 3.1 - HV Isolation Procedure
Before working on any HV component, the HV Level 2 technician must: switch the vehicle off and place the key at least 5 metres from the vehicle, disconnect the 12V battery, remove the HV service disconnect and apply a personal lock, wait at least 10 minutes or the time stated by the manufacturer if longer, and verify absence of voltage using the live-dead-live method with a CAT III 1000V rated meter.

### [DOC-005] Section 3.2 - Insulated Gloves
Class 0 insulated gloves must be worn for HV work. Gloves must be air-tested by the user before each use and electrically tested by an approved lab every 6 months.

### [DOC-005] Section 4.1 - Engine Oil (Hybrids Only)
Battery electric vehicles have no engine oil requirement. Hybrid and plug-in hybrid vehicles follow the engine oil interval in DOC-001.

### [DOC-005] Section 4.2 - Brake Inspection Interval for EVs and Hybrids
Because regenerative braking reduces friction brake wear, brakes on electric and hybrid vehicles must be inspected every 30,000 km or 12 months, whichever comes first.

### [DOC-005] Section 4.3 - Brake Caliper Service
Brake caliper slide pins must be cleaned and lubricated every 12 months, because low friction-brake use on electric and hybrid vehicles increases the risk of corrosion and seizing.

### [DOC-005] Section 4.4 - Battery Thermal Management Coolant
Battery coolant level must be inspected at every PM service. Battery coolant replacement follows the manufacturer's schedule.

### [DOC-005] Section 4.5 - Tires on Electric Vehicles
Because electric vehicles are heavier, tire tread depth must be measured every 10,000 km. Tread depth replacement limits follow the Brake and Tire Safety Standard (DOC-002).

### [DOC-005] Section 4.6 - HV Battery Health Check
An HV battery state-of-health (SoH) check must be performed every 12 months. Any battery with SoH below 70% must be escalated to Fleet Engineering for a replacement decision.

### [DOC-005] Section 5 - Charging Equipment
Depot charging stations and cables must be visually inspected monthly. Damaged cables or connectors must be tagged out of use immediately.

### [DOC-005] Section 6 - Damaged or Crashed EVs
An electric or hybrid vehicle with HV battery damage or that has been in a significant collision must be parked outdoors at least 15 metres from buildings and other vehicles and monitored for 48 hours before entering the workshop.

### [DOC-005] Section 7 - HV Work Records
Each HV isolation must be logged in FleetTrack with the technician name, certification number, date, and verification reading.
